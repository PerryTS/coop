Bounded parallel preload did not materially improve this macOS 100-image case;
dyld eager relocation remains effectively serialized. A separate diagnostic
loaded 20 tiny unique images in 6645 ms with RTLD_NOW versus 200 ms with

the first request and violate the prewarmed execution contract.
